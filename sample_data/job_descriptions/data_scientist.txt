Data Scientist

About the Role
We are hiring a Data Scientist to join our Analytics team. You will leverage statistical methods and machine learning to extract insights from large datasets and build predictive models that drive business decisions.

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience in data science or a related quantitative field
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL
- Experience with machine learning libraries (scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch)
- Solid foundation in statistics and probability
- Experience with data manipulation and analysis tools (Pandas, NumPy)
- Experience with data visualization (Matplotlib, Seaborn, Tableau, or Power BI)
- Experience working with large datasets and databases (PostgreSQL, BigQuery, or similar)
- Master's degree or PhD in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or related field

Nice to Have
- Experience with deep learning and NLP
- Knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS SageMaker, GCP Vertex AI)
- Experience with A/B testing and experimentation frameworks
- Familiarity with Apache Spark or other big data tools
- Published research or conference presentations
- Experience with MLOps and model deployment

Responsibilities
- Develop and deploy machine learning models to solve business problems
- Analyze large, complex datasets to identify patterns, trends, and opportunities
- Design and analyze A/B tests to measure the impact of product changes
- Build data pipelines and automate reporting workflows
- Communicate findings to stakeholders through clear visualizations and presentations
- Collaborate with engineering teams to integrate models into production systems
- Stay current with the latest developments in data science and machine learning
